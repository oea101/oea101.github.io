Hi, I’m Manny Akintayo. I’m an engineer by training, a product leader, and an entrepreneur. My work spans AI, healthcare, fintech, and consumer products. I’ve built new businesses and helped scale products used by millions of people. Today, I lead AI product and platform teams at PayPal.

I started my career building software for banking and healthcare, working on distributed applications and enterprise search systems. At OfferUp and Meta, I worked on machine learning infrastructure for advertising, including data pipelines for ad ranking and serving that helped generate $1 billion in new revenue.

I later co-founded Gentem Health, combining AI and financial services to simplify medical billing and help doctors get paid. We raised $20 million across several funding rounds and scaled the product and business before selling the company to a publicly traded company in 2023.

After Gentem, I joined Menlo Ventures as an Entrepreneur in Residence, exploring new startup ideas.

I enjoy staying close to the technology while thinking about the people and problems behind it. This site is where I share my writing, conversations, and experiments in AI and product building.
